# Whaleora photography direction

## Emotional brief

Photograph confidence, movement and everyday preparedness. Never stage fear, pursuit, defensive poses or a woman looking over her shoulder.

## Product

- Macro details, clean silhouettes and tactile material closeups.
- Warm tonal surfaces: cream paper, sand, pale stone, brushed metal and terracotta.
- Products attached naturally to keys, tote bags, backpacks and travel pouches.
- Top-down everyday-carry compositions with real personal objects.
- Directional soft light with honest shadows; avoid levitating-product clichés.

## Lifestyle

- Indian urban settings: metro concourses, campuses, cafés, cabs, airports, evening commutes and workplaces.
- People are mid-action—walking, packing, meeting friends, leaving a class or arriving home.
- Styling is contemporary, credible and individual rather than overtly aspirational.
- Prefer morning/evening natural light and architectural depth.

## Framing

- Mix broad environmental frames with close hand/product details.
- Leave intentional negative space for interface copy where required.
- Use 4:5, 3:2 and wide 16:9 crops with safe subject placement.

## Avoid

- Frightened expressions, deserted alley clichés, red emergency lighting.
- Weapons, tactical clothing, police imagery or staged confrontation.
- Generic Western city markers when the campaign is India-focused.
- Excessive pink, beauty-editorial retouching or unrealistic skin.
