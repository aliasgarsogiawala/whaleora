# Creative direction — Preparedness, beautifully carried

## Concept

Whaleora becomes an editorial personal-safety lifestyle brand: warm enough to feel human, precise enough to feel dependable, and restrained enough for its products to command attention.

The page rhythm moves through four modes:

1. **Everyday life** — cinematic movement and confident Indian urban contexts.
2. **Everyday objects** — oversized real product photography and material closeups.
3. **Everyday knowledge** — magazine-like guides, personas and resources.
4. **Everyday community** — founder, institutions and an inviting movement CTA.

## Colour system

- Ink / primary text: `#102844`
- Deep navy: `#0A1B2F`
- Warm ivory: `#F8ECDE`
- Paper: `#FFF9F1`
- Terracotta: `#D7673D`
- Dark terracotta: `#8F351B`
- Sand: `#DDBB98`
- Sage: `#748A83`
- Hairline: `rgba(16,40,68,.16)`

Colour is used in broad editorial fields, not repeated rounded containers. Terracotta remains a precise accent for actions and small brand moments.

## Typography

- Display/editorial serif: **DM Serif Display** for a human, authored voice.
- Interface/body: **Manrope** for compact, modern clarity.
- Large headings use tight leading and fluid `clamp()` sizing; micro labels use upper-case Manrope with generous tracking.

## Grid and spacing

- Max content width: 1540px.
- Twelve-column desktop grid; asymmetric 7/5 and 8/4 compositions.
- Full-bleed colour and image sections interrupt the container rhythm.
- Section spacing is fluid from 72px mobile to 150px desktop.

## Component language

- Square and softly rounded corners (8–24px), never a page of identical pills.
- Thin navy hairlines, restrained shadows and strong image cropping.
- Editorial index numbers and oversized words replace generic icon grids.
- Buttons are compact, high-contrast and animated with directional arrows.

## Motion

- CSS-first reveal and hover transitions.
- Product images drift only a few pixels; no theatrical floating.
- Sticky/scroll effects are limited to the hero/nav and product feature story.
- All transitions are removed or reduced for `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Photography

- Real Whaleora product and founder assets lead.
- One original generated lifestyle scene expands the available visual world without faking a Whaleora product photograph.
- Future shoot direction prioritises Indian urban independence and everyday movement rather than alarmed expressions.
